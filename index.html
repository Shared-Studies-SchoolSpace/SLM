    How the mapping works

    道 ( dào , the Word )
    Hanzi Pinyin English
    • Occurrence-order based. Each entry maps to one occurrence in the text — the same Hanzi appearing twice needs two entries, in sequence.
    • Progressive disclosure. Toggle Pinyin above the passage, or click any character to pop up its Pinyin + English.

    Why learner-controlled?

    Permanently displaying Pinyin and English lets the eye skip the characters. By keeping assistance hidden until the moment you request it, HanziNA keeps the Hanzi as the primary representation — you decide when help is disclosed.
